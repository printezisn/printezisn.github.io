Building Capture One’s new data warehouse on Azure, along with the Power BI reporting that every department uses to make decisions, up to the C-suite. One of three engineers on a team with an engineering manager and a PM.

- Rebuilt the company’s data warehouse from scratch to replace the legacy system, **cutting daily data ingestion from ~8 hours to ~30 minutes (16× faster)**.
- Built automated checks that reconcile financial data against official financial reports and stop bad data before it reaches reports, with alerts for investigation. This **earned leadership’s trust in the numbers**.
- Helped move the company from decisions made on scarce data to **data as the main driver of decisions**, with reports used by ~10 teams across every department, from engineering PMs and marketing to the C-suite.
- **Built Claude skills that open the data warehouse to non-technical colleagues**: one detects schema changes and **drafts glossary descriptions and personal-data sensitivity flags for human review**, cutting the monthly glossary update (used by analysts and the **legal team**) from **~6 hours to ~1 hour**. Another helps analysts **write new analysis queries by reusing a library of past analyses**.
